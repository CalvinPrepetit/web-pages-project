The Mercer Family

Meet the Family

A modern family of five from Charlotte with Puerto Rican, Lebanese American, Italian American, and long-time Southern American roots. They stay busy with sports, school clubs, creative work, travel, and community events.

A close family that loves outdoor trips, game days, creative work, and celebrating a blend of Puerto Rican and deeply rooted American family traditions.
